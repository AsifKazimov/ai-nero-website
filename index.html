Product vision

An integrated AI-powered audit workspace

The project aims to support audit professionals and finance teams with structured, reproducible workflows. Capabilities below describe development goals and areas under exploration, not a promise of production availability.

▤
Document intelligence

Extract, organize, and cross-check information from invoices, contracts, and financial documents.

⌁
Audit automation

Assist with reconciliations, exception tracking, and evidence-linked financial analysis.

◇
Agent-based workflows

Coordinate retrieval, calculation, validation, and human review with clear responsibility boundaries.

Technical approach

Reasoning is useful. Verification is essential.

AI Nero is being designed around an evidence-first workflow. Language models can assist with understanding and planning, while deterministic logic handles calculation and policy checks.

Design principle: AI-generated outputs should not be treated as verified audit conclusions without source evidence, applicable rules, and review.
Evidence collectionSource tracking, document references, and lineage
Rule-based checksStructured policies and precise financial calculations
ReconciliationConsistency checks between records and documents
Human oversightTransparent exceptions and auditable decisions

About AI Nero

Independently developed. Built for financial rigor.
